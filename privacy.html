What 17-0 Draft is

17-0 Draft is a free fan-made football draft game. It is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL, the NFL Players Association, any team, or any player. Player names and career statistics are public facts; team colors and abbreviations are used for identification only. No logos are used.

Playing without an account

You can play without signing in. Your game data (drafts, records, your Ring of Honor collection, your look preference) is stored only on your device, in your browser's or the app's local storage. We never see it.

If you create an account

Signing in with your email lets your game data follow you across devices. We then store:

  • your email address (used only to send the sign-in code and identify your account),
  • a random account id,
  • your game data: records, Ring of Honor collection, and your best runs for the leaderboard,
  • the display name you choose, which is shown on the shared leaderboard.

This data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than running the game.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, no in-app purchases.
  • No contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos access.
  • No messages between players, no user-generated content other than a display name.

Deleting your account

Open Account in the game and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your email, account id, display name and cloud game data from our servers. Data stored on your own device stays until you clear it.

Children

The game is rated for ages 4+ and collects no personal data unless you choose to create an account with an email address.
