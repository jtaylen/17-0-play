17-0 Draft · Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

What 17-0 Draft is

17-0 Draft is a free fan-made football draft game. It is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL, the NFL Players Association, any team, or any player. Player names and career statistics are public facts; team colors and abbreviations are used for identification only. No logos are used.

Playing without an account

You can play without signing in. Your game data (drafts, records, your Ring of Honor collection, your look preference) stays on your device, in your browser's or the app's local storage. The only thing that leaves it is the anonymous play stats described below.

Play stats

As you play, the game sends us a few anonymous notes about the play itself: a random device id minted on your device, which modes and features you use, your records and draft times, and how long each session lasts. No name, no email, no location, nothing you type. We use it only to see what people play and for how long, so the game gets better where it matters. To have it deleted, send us the first 8 characters of your device id, shown at the bottom of the Leaderboards screen, and we remove every row for that id.

If you create an account

An account is optional. Signing in with Apple lets your game data follow you across devices and opens the global leaderboards, Chat, Ideas, Friends and messages. We then store:

  • the email address Apple shares with us, which can be a private relay address if you choose to hide yours, and Apple's user id for your account,
  • a random account id,
  • your game data: records, Ring of Honor collection, your best runs for the leaderboards, your XP and rank, and your trivia rating,
  • the board name you choose, which other players see on the leaderboards, in Chat, in Ideas and in Friends.

Chat and Ideas

Chat is one room for every signed-in player and Ideas is a public suggestion list. What you post there is shown to other players under your board name and is stored with your account. Every post passes a word filter; links and phone numbers are refused. Any player can report a post or block another player, and we remove posts and mute accounts that break the rules. Chat is for players 13 and older.

Friends and private messages (iPhone app)

If you use Friends, we store your friend code, your friend requests, who your friends are and who you have blocked. Players are added only by friend code; there is no player search and the game never reads your phone's contacts.

Private messages go only between two players who are friends. They are stored on our servers so both of you can read them, and they pass the same word filter as Chat. We do not read private messages. The one exception is a message a player reports: a copy of that message, with the sender's board name, is kept for moderation, and that copy stays even if an account is later deleted. Messages are for players 13 and older.

Notifications (iPhone app)

Notifications are off until you say yes. If you allow them, we store your phone's notification token with your account and use it only to tell you about a reply to you in Chat, a message from a friend or a friend request. A notification names the other player and never includes the message text. We send no marketing notifications. Turning them off in Account removes the token. You can also turn them off in your phone's Settings.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States. Notifications are delivered by Apple. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than running the game.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, no in-app purchases.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Deleting your account

Open Account in the game and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your email, account id, board name, cloud game data, chat messages, ideas, friend code, friendships, private messages and notification token from our servers. The copy of a message that another player reported is kept, as described above. Data stored on your own device stays until you clear it.

Children

The game can be played without an account and without giving us any personal information. Chat and private messages are for players 13 and older, and the game asks you to confirm that before you can use them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.
