Games like 17-0

Last updated November 3, 2026

Spin a team and an era, draft legends, go undefeated: by late 2026 that hook has a whole shelf of games. Here is where 17-0: Draft a Perfect Season sits next to the ones people ask about, one honest line each.

Retro Bowl

The pixel football management sim that defined the look: run a franchise season after season, manage the roster and call the plays. Free on the App Store with in-app purchases. 17-0 shares the retro spirit but is a draft game, not a sim you manage for years: a run takes minutes.

20-0: Sports Draft Legends

A perfect-season roster builder across ten sports, with tournaments, ranked play and friends. Free with in-app purchases. 17-0 is football only, with a pixel look, daily trivia and a collection to build, and nothing to buy.

82-0 Challenge

The basketball version of the hook: draw a team and a decade, draft a legend, chase an 82-0 season. Free with in-app purchases. 17-0 is the football game of the same idea, free with no ads and no purchases.

84-0

A set of sports draft games, including a build-a-player mode and live 1v1 head-to-head, where one loss ends the run. Free on the App Store. 17-0 is single-player and offline, and keeps every perfect Packs roster in your Ring of Honor.

Gridiron Trivia

A daily NFL trivia app with many modes, from grid puzzles and Career Path to its own 17-0 draft mode. Free with in-app purchases. 17-0 pairs its daily trivia (TD Trivia and NFL Trivia) with a full draft-and-season game, free with no purchases.

Immaculate Grid

The daily nine-square grid: name a player who fits each pair of teams, or a team and a career stat, nine guesses, one grid a day, shared as a score. Free on the web. 17-0's daily games are a drive of touchdown questions, an all-time-lists quiz and a timed Hard draft, and they live inside the same free app as the season game.

What 17-0 does differently

  • Pixel look: a retro pixel field and pixel player models, with a Modern broadcast look if you prefer it.
  • Free, no ads, no in-app purchases: packs are free and unlimited, nothing is bought or wagered.
  • Daily trivia: TD Trivia, NFL Trivia and Daily 17-0, a fresh set every day.
  • Ring of Honor: go 17-0 in Packs and the eligible players on that roster are yours for good, 1,319 collectible players.
  • iPhone and web: the App Store app and the browser version are the same game; it plays offline and needs no account.
