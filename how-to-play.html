How to play 17-0

Last updated November 3, 2026

17-0: Draft a Perfect Season is a free football draft game for iPhone and the web. Spin a team and a decade, draft the best players you can find, then play a 17-game season and see if you go undefeated. This page describes what the game does today.

The loop

  1. Pick a mode and a difficulty on the Home screen.
  2. Spin: the reels land on a team and a decade. Your next pick must have played for that team in that decade.
  3. Draft a player for the spot. Repeat until the roster is full.
  4. Play the season and watch every game play out.

Three ways to draft

  • Classic: a QB, skill players, an O-Line unit and a Defense unit.
  • Full Roster: all 22 starters, a kicker and a head coach, one pick at a time.
  • Packs: open a 6-card pack for every Classic spot and a head coach, and keep one card. Open the coach first; his style shapes every pick after. Boosts reward teammates who really played together, a scheme that fits, a coach who fits and a line that protects. On Medium and Hard the pack keeps its secrets: no peeking at the Legend until you keep it.

Three difficulties

  • Easy shows ratings, stats and playing years.
  • Medium shows ratings and playing years.
  • Hard shows names only. You have to know your football history.

Spins, re-spins and filters

At every pick you can re-spin the team and the decade separately: three of each on Easy, two on Medium, one on Hard. A re-spin keeps the other reel. Before you start a Classic or Full Roster draft you can narrow the draw: only the decades you choose, the AFC, the NFC or a single division.

The season

Every game plays out on a retro pixel field or in a TV-style broadcast look, your choice. Go 17-0 in the regular season, then sweep the playoffs for the title. Afterwards the game shows the best possible team your spins allowed, so every loss teaches you something. Packs rewards a perfect title season with a God Pack, and three perfect seasons in a row with a Dynasty Pack.

Ring of Honor and My Team

Go 17-0 in Packs and every eligible player on that roster is banked in your Ring of Honor for good: 1,319 collectible players with pixel models, rare Legend variants (Moment, Rookie, Throwback, Farewell and Mythic cards) and Hall of Famer badges, with a card view you can flip and tilt. My Team puts six favorites from your Ring on display with their own rating.

Daily 17-0

One Classic draft a day on Hard: names only, fresh spins on every try, three tries a day. A try is spent the moment it starts, so quitting keeps nothing. The clock runs while you search and place, never while the reels spin. Your best try counts, the record first and then the clock, on the global Daily 17-0 board under your board name.

TD Trivia

A seven-play drive every day. Six prompts in a fresh order, about real touchdowns from 1999 on and the rosters of anyone who played in 1980 or later, then the two-minute drill: name a player by his nickname, the rarer the better. Answer with anyone in the game. You get 25 seconds a play and 60 for the drill. Start at your own 25; a right answer gains yards. Score a touchdown for 7, stall inside the 35 for a field goal for 3, or punt. TD Trivia ELO is a rating that climbs as you prove your football knowledge, with questions that adapt to you.

NFL Trivia

Seven questions a day from the NFL's all-time lists: the top 10 in passing, rushing, receiving, touchdowns, sacks and interceptions, plus four-time Super Bowl winners, players with eight or more franchises and the Hall of Fame by position. Name one player on the list; you get one guess. The better known the name, the fewer the points: the most famous answer on a list is worth 1 point, the deepest cut 10, a miss 0. After every guess the whole list shows. A perfect day is 70.

Looks, leaderboards and saves

Switch between the Retro pixel look and the Modern broadcast look any time. Every mode has a leaderboard on your device; Daily 17-0 has a global board. No account is needed; your progress is saved on your device, and the iPhone app plays fully offline.
