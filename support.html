Questions, bugs, or ideas? Email johnataylen@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug.

Your progress

Without an account, the game saves progress on your device only, and deleting the app deletes it. With the optional account (Sign in with Apple) your Ring of Honor, records and rank are saved to the cloud and follow you to a new phone. There are no purchases and no ads.

Report a player, a post or a message

Every chat post, idea and private message from another player has a Report button, and you can block any player from a chat message or from their friend page. You can also write to the address above. We read reports every day and act within 24 hours.

Delete your account

In the game: Home, Account, Delete account. It removes your account and its cloud data at once. If you cannot open the game, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
