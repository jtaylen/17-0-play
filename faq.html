17-0: Draft a Perfect Season FAQ

Last updated November 3, 2026

Straight answers about 17-0: Draft a Perfect Season, the free football draft game for iPhone and the web.

What is 17-0: Draft a Perfect Season?

17-0 is a free football draft game for iPhone and the web, made by one developer, John Taylen. Spin a team and a decade, draft NFL legends from every era, then play a 17-game season and try to go undefeated. It is an unofficial fan game, not affiliated with the NFL, NFLPA, any team or player.

Is 17-0 free?

Yes. It is free on the App Store and free to play in the browser. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Packs are free and unlimited; nothing is bought or wagered.

Is 17-0 on Android?

Not today. 17-0 is an iPhone app on the App Store, and it plays in any modern browser at jtaylen.github.io/17-0-play, including the browser on an Android phone. There is no Android app.

Does the game use real players?

Yes. Thousands of real players from the 1940s to today appear with their real teams, playing years and career statistics, which are public facts. Team colors and abbreviations identify teams; no league or team logos are used. 17-0 is an unofficial fan game.

How is 17-0 different from fantasy football?

Fantasy football follows the live NFL season week by week, scoring real games inside a league of friends. 17-0 is a quick single-player game you finish in minutes: the players you draft come from every era, the team and the decade come from a spin, and the 17-game season plays out inside the game. No leagues, no live scoring, no money.

How do I go 17-0?

Fill every spot with the best players your spins allow, and use your re-spins when a team and decade give you nothing. On Easy the game shows ratings, stats and playing years, so compare the numbers. Medium hides the stats, and Hard shows names only, so you have to know your football history. After every season the game shows the best possible team your spins allowed, so every loss teaches you something.

What is the Ring of Honor?

The Ring of Honor is the Packs mode collection. Go 17-0 in Packs and every eligible player on that roster is banked in your Ring of Honor for good: 1,319 collectible players with pixel models, rare Legend variants (Moment, Rookie, Throwback, Farewell and Mythic cards) and Hall of Famer badges. My Team puts six favorites from your Ring on display.

What is Daily TD Trivia?

TD Trivia is a seven-play trivia drive every day. Six prompts about real touchdowns from 1999 on and the rosters of anyone who played in 1980 or later, then a two-minute drill: name a player by his nickname, the rarer the better. You can answer with anyone in the game. A right answer gains yards; score a touchdown for 7, stall inside the 35 for a field goal, or punt. TD Trivia ELO is a rating that climbs as you prove your football knowledge, with questions that adapt to you.

What is Daily 17-0?

One Classic draft a day on Hard: names only, fresh spins on every try, three tries a day. Your best try counts, the record first and then the clock, and it goes to the global Daily 17-0 board under your board name.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without signing in. Your drafts, records and Ring of Honor are saved on your device.

Does 17-0 work offline?

Yes. The iPhone app plays fully offline. The web version saves itself for offline play after the first visit. The global Daily 17-0 board needs a connection.

Can I play 17-0 on the web?

Yes, at jtaylen.github.io/17-0-play, the same game as the App Store version. On a phone, add it to your Home Screen for a full-screen, app-like game.
